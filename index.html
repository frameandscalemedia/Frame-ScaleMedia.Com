00:07 — Questions

Frequently asked questions.

How much does a videographer cost for a small business in Phoenix?

Our monthly reels packages are $1,500 for 4 reels, $2,750 for 8 reels and $4,000 for 12 reels per month. One-time projects: testimonial or case study video $500, brand and promo video $500 to $1,500, website or landing page video $500 to $1,000, hiring video $500, a full photography shoot day $500, real estate video $1,000 per listing, and event coverage $1,000 to $3,000.

Where does Frame and Scale Media work?

We are a mobile team. We come to your location or help find locations for the shoot across Phoenix, Peoria, Scottsdale, Surprise, Mesa, Chandler and the rest of the Phoenix area.

What are your business hours?

We are available 7am to 7pm, Monday through Sunday.

Do you make Facebook and Instagram ad creative?

Yes. Paid ad creative starts at $1,500 for 3 high-converting ad creatives. If you want us to manage the Meta ads, our management fee matches your ad spend 100%. For example, $500 of ad spend costs $1,000 total, and the other $500 covers Meta management.

Do you post the content for us?

The reels packages cover creating the content. Social media management and posting is available for an additional $1,000 per month on top of your retainer, depending on the bundle.

How much does event videography cost in Phoenix?

Event coverage runs $1,000 to $3,000, depending on the size of the event, how much video you need and the deliverables.

Do you do real estate video and drone photography?

Yes. Real estate packages are $1,000 per listing and include 3 social media videos plus full camera and drone photography coverage.
