FRAME AND SCALE MEDIA  /  PORTFOLIO VIDEOS
=========================================

Every video on the site lives in this folder (assets/portfolio).
All of them were compressed for the web (720p, about 2 to 10 MB each), so pages load fast.

  name.mp4           the video
  name.jpg           its still frame, shown until someone presses play
  style_preview.mp4  an 8 second silent loop that plays on the home page card for that style

ADDING A NEW VIDEO LATER
  1. Export at 1080p or smaller as .mp4 and drop it in this folder with a short name, no spaces.
  2. Open the style's page (for example automotive.html), copy one of the <figure> blocks in the
     Examples section, and change the file name and the caption.
  Or just send the video to Claude and ask to add it.
